# OLFACTUS v1.6.0b — Collection Value Dashboard

Model: `CVD-1.0.0`

Adds:
- `/market` portfolio workspace
- estimated market and retail replacement values
- acquisition cost and savings
- average cost per wear
- Market Health and portfolio grade
- brand and family allocation
- top holdings
- best purchases
- needs-attention ranking
- diversification, concentration, replacement, underuse, and stability risk
- AI Market Analyst
- compact Today-page market widget

Market values are calibrated seed estimates in this release. Live external
market feeds are planned for a later market-data integration phase.
