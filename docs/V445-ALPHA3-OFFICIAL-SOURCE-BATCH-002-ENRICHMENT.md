- Yves Saint Laurent

- Valentino
- Initio

- Le Labo
- Diptyque
- Byredo
- Essential Parfums

Combined with Batch 001, the sourced pilot universe reaches 89 records across
20 houses.

## Enrichment Queue

Every Catalog V2 record is scored for metadata completeness. Missing
release year, concentration, family, perfumer, notes, accords, collections,
and country fields become explicit enrichment tasks.

Tasks are prioritized as:

- critical
- high
- medium
- low

This lets OLFACTUS scale catalog breadth without pretending incomplete records
are equally intelligent.

## Trust boundary

Batch 002 creates zero intelligence profiles by design. It does not invent:

- DNA
- seasons
- roles
- performance
- moods

Therefore newly sourced records remain outside NRE until a future calibrated
intelligence-enrichment workflow explicitly promotes them.
