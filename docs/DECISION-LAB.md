# OLFACTUS Decision Lab

Model: `DL-1.0.0`

The Decision Lab converts Discovery Intelligence into a direct purchase verdict.

## Outputs

- BUY / SAMPLE / SKIP
- purchase score
- decision confidence
- analyst report
- positive reasons
- watch reasons
- collection impact simulation
- overlap risk
- regret risk
- long-term ownership potential
- observed-price adjustment

## Dependencies

- Asset Registry
- Discovery Intelligence Engine
- Neural Recommendation System
