# OLFACTUS OS v3.1.0-alpha.1 — Predictive Intelligence Integration

The Predictive Simulator extends "what if I add this?" beyond immediate
collection scores.

It calculates:

- immediate Collection Health
- forecast Collection Health range
- redundancy delta
- diversity delta
- role coverage delta
- estimated wears per month
- signature potential
- neglect risk
- retention probability
- confidence
- future verdict

Projection horizons: 30 days, 90 days, 6 months, and 1 year.

The real collection is never mutated by simulation. Sparse Memory evidence
reduces confidence and longer horizons widen uncertainty.
