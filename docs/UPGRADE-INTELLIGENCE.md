# OLFACTUS v1.9.0c — Upgrade Intelligence

Adds collection-aware intelligence inside the Lineage workspace.

## Upgrade Advisor

- upgrade score
- complement score
- replacement score
- buy confidence
- DNA separation
- collection gain
- performance gain
- role gain
- verdict and explanation

Verdicts:

- strong complement
- upgrade
- replacement
- redundant
- situational

## Family redundancy

- detects owned releases within the same fragrance line
- calculates pairwise family overlap
- calculates keep score for each owned release
- identifies highly redundant releases
- returns keep, optional, or consider-selling guidance
- explains the recommendation

The next phase, v1.9.0d, will integrate Lineage and Upgrade Intelligence into
Explorer, Deal Lab, Brand Intelligence, Perfumer Intelligence, and the
Knowledge Graph.
